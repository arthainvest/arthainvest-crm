⚙️ How Google Drive Integration Works

1️⃣

🔐 Connect Your Google Account

You authorize ArthaInvest to access your Google Drive. A secure folder "ArthaInvest_Documents" is created.

2️⃣

📤 Upload Documents

Upload client documents (PAN, Aadhar, bank statements, etc.) from your computer. They go directly to Google Drive.

3️⃣

📋 Track in ArthaInvest

Each document is recorded in ArthaInvest with a Google Drive ID. You always know where the file is.

4️⃣

🔍 Search & Retrieve

Find any document instantly from ArthaInvest. Download or share with team members right from the CRM.

5️⃣

📊 Export & Backup

Export all document metadata as JSON for backup. If you change systems, you keep your records and links.

📚 Real-World Example

Scenario: You're managing insurance applications. Each applicant needs to submit PAN, Aadhar, and bank statements.

What you do:
1. Client calls and sends you photos of PAN and Aadhar
2. You log the client in ArthaInvest
3. Upload both documents → automatically go to Google Drive
4. Documents are tagged with Google Drive IDs

Next week:
1. Client asks for a copy of their submitted documents
2. Open ArthaInvest, search the client
3. Click "Download" → file opens from Google Drive
4. Send to client instantly

Month later:
1. Need to migrate to a different CRM
2. Export all documents as JSON
3. Import into new system → all Google Drive links work!

Result: Zero data loss, always accessible, professional document management! 💯
