⭐ FEATURE #3: LEAD SCORING

WHAT IT DOES:
  Automatically score each lead from 0-100
  Show which leads are most likely to convert
  Prioritize your follow-ups
  Make data-driven decisions about lead quality

SCORING FACTORS (Auto-calculated):
  1. Budget Amount (up to 30 points)
     • Higher budget = Higher score
     • ₹100K budget = 30 points
     • ₹50K budget = 15 points

  2. Lead Status (up to 40 points)
     • New: 5 points
     • Contacted: 15 points
     • Interested: 25 points
     • Meeting: 35 points
     • Closed: 40 points

  3. Recent Contact (up to 20 points)
     • Just contacted today = 20 points
     • Contacted 1 week ago = 15 points
     • Contacted 2+ weeks ago = lower points

  4. Documents Uploaded (up to 10 points)
     • Each document = 2 points
     • 5 documents = 10 points

TOTAL SCORE: 0-100

HOW TO USE:
  1. Click "⭐ Lead Scoring" in sidebar
  2. See all leads with their scores
  3. Scores show:
     • GREEN (80-100): Hot leads, prioritize these!
     • YELLOW (50-79): Warm leads, follow up soon
     • RED (0-49): Cold leads, nurture for later

EXAMPLE SCORES:
  Rajesh Kumar: 87/100 (HOT - Budget: 500K, Interested, Recent contact)
  Priya Singh: 45/100 (COLD - Budget: 100K, New, No contact)
  Amit Patel: 92/100 (VERY HOT - Budget: 1M, Meeting scheduled, Active)

BENEFITS:
  ✅ Know which leads to call first
  ✅ Prioritize your time
  ✅ Focus on high-value leads
  ✅ Track lead quality over time
  ✅ Improve conversion rate
