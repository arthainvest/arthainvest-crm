## 🎯 LAUNCH OVERVIEW

### **Objective**
Deploy a complete, automated CRM system to ArthaInvest sales team to increase efficiency by 50%+ and automate 70% of administrative tasks.

### **Scope**
- ✅ 15-sheet CRM with real data
- ✅ 5 automation phases (Notifications, Workflows, Scoring, Sequences, Communication)
- ✅ Google Sheets + Google Apps Script (zero cost)
- ✅ 27 pre-loaded clients + 12 prospects
- ✅ 4 products (Tata, Niva, POSP, DSA)
- ✅ Complete team training

### **Timeline**
- **Phase 1 (Days 1-2):** Pre-launch & setup
- **Phase 2 (Days 3-7):** Team training & onboarding
- **Phase 3 (Week 2):** Full adoption & monitoring
- **Phase 4 (Week 3-4):** Optimization & refinement

### **Expected ROI**
- 55% reduction in time per lead
- 100% increase in leads per person per month
- 40% improvement in conversion rate
- **Year 1 Revenue Impact: ₹12-16.8 Crore additional revenue**

## ⚠️ RISK MANAGEMENT

### **RISK 1: Data Quality Issues**

**Risk:** Team enters wrong data → Bad scoring
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Pre-training validation rules
- ✅ Daily audit first week
- ✅ Edit access restrictions
- ✅ Approval process for bulk changes

**Owner:** CRM Owner

---

### **RISK 2: Low Adoption**

**Risk:** Team doesn't use CRM → No ROI
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Daily standup (peer pressure)
- ✅ Celebrate wins loudly
- ✅ Make it easy (laminated guide)
- ✅ Remove old system day 5

**Owner:** CRM Owner + Team

---

### **RISK 3: System Errors**

**Risk:** CRM crashes → Lost productivity
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Backup copies (Excel)
- ✅ Google Sheet auto-saves
- ✅ Test all phases before launch
- ✅ 24/7 Google Sheets support

**Owner:** CRM Owner

---

### **RISK 4: Workflow Failures**

**Risk:** Workflows don't trigger → Manual work needed
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Test all workflows pre-launch
- ✅ Fallback to manual if needed
- ✅ Log failures to troubleshoot
- ✅ Have workarounds ready

**Owner:** CRM Owner

---

### **RISK 5: Integration Issues**

**Risk:** WhatsApp/Email API doesn't connect
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Phase 1: System works without API
- ✅ Phase 2: Add API after team comfortable
- ✅ Have manual fallback
- ✅ Gradual rollout

**Owner:** CRM Owner
