📥 INSTALLATION

Step 1: Receive the Installer
  • Your manager will send: arthainvest-crm-setup.exe (64.81 MB)
  • Via: Email, OneDrive, Google Drive, USB, or Network share

Step 2: Run the Installer
  1. Double-click: arthainvest-crm-setup.exe
  2. Windows SmartScreen may appear
     → Click "More info" → Click "Run anyway"
  3. Installation wizard opens
  4. Click "Next" on each screen
  5. Click "Install" when ready
  6. Wait 2-3 minutes for installation
  7. Click "Finish" (Launch app should be checked)

Step 3: CRM Opens Automatically
  • First launch may take 5-10 seconds
  • You'll see the login screen

📁 DOCUMENTS FEATURE (NEW!)

The Documents feature lets you store client files organized by folder:

How to Use:
  1. Click "Documents" in left sidebar
  2. Select a client/lead
  3. Click "+ Add Document"
  4. Enter document name (e.g., "PAN Card")
  5. Select document type from dropdown:
     • PAN Card
     • Aadhaar
     • Insurance Policy
     • Bank Statement
     • Agreement
     • Invoice
     • Other
  6. Click "Upload Document"
  7. Document saved and appears in list

Each document shows:
  • Name of document
  • Type
  • Upload date/time
  • Who uploaded it
  • Delete button if needed

📱 MANAGING LEADS

Add a New Lead:
  1. Click "All Leads" in sidebar
  2. Click "+ Add Lead"
  3. Fill in the form:
     • Name (required)
     • Phone
     • Email
     • Status
     • Budget
     • Other fields as needed
  4. Click "Save Lead"

Edit an Existing Lead:
  1. Click "All Leads"
  2. Click on the lead name
  3. Update the information
  4. Click "Save Lead"

View Lead Status:
  • New - Not yet contacted
  • Contacted - You've reached out
  • Interested - Client showed interest
  • Meeting - Scheduled meeting
  • Closed - Deal closed or disqualified

🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Installer won't run
  Solution:
    • Restart your computer
    • Try running as Administrator (right-click installer)
    • Check antivirus isn't blocking it

Problem: SmartScreen warning appears
  Solution:
    • Click "More info"
    • Click "Run anyway"
    • This is normal for new software

Problem: App won't start after installation
  Solution:
    • Restart your computer
    • Look for "ArthaInvest CRM" in Start Menu
    • Or check: C:\Program Files\ArthaInvest CRM\

Problem: Login fails
  Solution:
    • Verify username: artha (all lowercase)
    • Verify password: artha123
    • No spaces before or after
    • Contact your manager for credential updates

Problem: Can't see assigned leads
  Solution:
    • Refresh page (F5)
    • Restart app
    • Contact your manager - they need to assign leads
