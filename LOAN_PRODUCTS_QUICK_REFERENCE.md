## 📄 DOCUMENT DEFINITIONS

### **PAN Card**
- Permanent Account Number issued by Income Tax Department
- Proof of identity and income tax registration
- Valid for 10 years

### **Aadhar Card**
- Unique 12-digit identity number issued by UIDAI
- Biometric and demographic data
- Lifetime validity

### **Bank Statement**
- Official record of account transactions
- Stamped/digitally verified by bank
- Required duration varies by product

### **Income Proof**
- Salary slip (salaried)
- ITR/Tax return (business)
- Pension letter (retired)

### **ITR (Income Tax Return)**
- Filed with Income Tax Department
- Proof of income and financial status
- Last 2-3 years required

### **Property Documents**
- Sale deed/Registration certificate
- Latest tax receipts
- Site plan
- Occupancy certificate (if applicable)

### **Business Documents**
- Registration certificate
- GST certificate
- Memorandum & Articles of Association
- Board resolutions
