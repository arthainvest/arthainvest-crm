✅ METHOD 2: File Explorer (Direct Path)

STEP 1: Open File Explorer
  • Click the folder icon on your taskbar
  • OR press Windows + E

STEP 2: Copy the Path
  Copy this entire path:

  C:\Program Files\ArthaInvest CRM\

  (Or you can type it)

STEP 3: Paste in Address Bar
  • Click the address bar at the top
  • Right-click and paste
  • Press Enter

STEP 4: Open the Executable
  • You'll see: ArthaInvest CRM.exe
  • Double-click it
  • App opens in 2-3 seconds
