# Loan Research

Rates, EMI numbers, and credit-bureau context all change; fetch live rather than answering from memory.

## EMI calculators / rate comparison
- **BankBazaar**, **Paisabazaar** - the standard India loan-comparison platforms; EMI calculators plus multi-lender rate comparison for home/personal/business/LAP loans.
- Individual lender's own EMI calculator (**SBI**, **HDFC**, **ICICI**, **Bajaj Finserv**, or whichever bank/NBFC a specific case is actually going to) - use this for the bindable number once a lender is chosen, since aggregator EMI figures are indicative.

## Credit bureau context
- **CIBIL**, **Experian**, **Equifax**, **CRIF Highmark** - the four bureaus operating in India; useful for explaining to a client what a credit score reflects, or for general score-improvement guidance (not for pulling an actual report - that requires the client's own consent/action, not something to do on their behalf).

## Official / regulatory
- **RBI** - the regulator; authoritative for repo rate (which drives floating-rate loan pricing), lending guidelines, and any circular affecting what can be offered.
- **NHB (National Housing Bank)** - home-loan-specific regulatory body.
- **SIDBI** - relevant for MSME/business loan cases, government scheme eligibility.

## News / market context
- **Moneycontrol** (Loans section) - lending market news, rate-change coverage.
- **Trading Economics** - useful for the broader interest-rate trend context behind why rates are moving.

## Workflow

1. For an **EMI estimate** during a prospect call: BankBazaar/Paisabazaar for a fast, multi-lender ballpark; the actual lender's own calculator once a specific loan product is being pursued (see `loan-documents`/`credit-manager` for the case-preparation side).
2. For **"why did rates move"**: RBI's repo rate announcements are the root cause for floating-rate products - check there first before a general market-news search.
3. For **credit-score conversations** with a client: explain in general terms using bureau sites' own educational content, but never attempt to pull or view a client's actual credit report without their explicit, separate consent through the proper channel - that's not something to do inside a CRM chat.
4. Cite source and date for any rate/EMI figure given to a client - these are point-in-time.
5. This is research support, not a lending decision or a substitute for the lender's own sanction process.
