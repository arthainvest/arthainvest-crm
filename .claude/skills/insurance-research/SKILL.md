# Insurance Research

Where to look depends on what's being asked - a premium quote, a claim-settlement track record, or a regulatory/product detail. Fetch live rather than relying on memory; premiums, IRDAI claim-settlement ratios, and product terms all change.

## Premium quotes / comparison platforms
- **Policybazaar** - the largest India insurance aggregator; the standard first stop for a multi-insurer premium comparison (life, health, motor).
- **Coverfox**, **Turtlemint** - alternate comparison platforms, useful for cross-checking a Policybazaar quote or when a client already has a relationship with one of these.

## Insurer-specific (this business's actual partners)
- **TATA AIG** / **Niva Bupa** - go to the insurer's own site for official product brochures, premium calculators, and claim forms for policies actually being placed with these partners, rather than relying on an aggregator's summary of their product.

## Official / regulatory
- **IRDAI** - the regulator; authoritative for claim-settlement ratios (published annually), product approval status, and any regulatory change that affects what can be sold or how.
- **IIB (Insurance Information Bureau)** - industry-wide claims data, useful for an independent check beyond what individual insurers self-report.

## News / market context
- **Moneycontrol** (Insurance section), **Livemint** (Insurance section) - industry news, new product launches, regulatory changes in plain language.

## Workflow

1. For a **premium quote** for a specific client: use Policybazaar/Coverfox/Turtlemint for a multi-insurer comparison, but go to TATA AIG/Niva Bupa directly when the policy will actually be placed there, since the aggregator's number is indicative, not the bindable quote.
2. For **claim-settlement confidence** (a client asking "will they actually pay out"): IRDAI's published ratios are the authoritative source, not an insurer's own marketing claim.
3. For **"what changed"** questions: check Moneycontrol/Livemint's insurance sections and IRDAI's own announcements.
4. Cite the source and date for anything premium- or ratio-related given to a client - these figures are point-in-time and the client may reasonably ask when it was checked.
5. This is research support, not underwriting or a substitute for the insurer's own quote engine - present findings, let the human place the actual policy.
