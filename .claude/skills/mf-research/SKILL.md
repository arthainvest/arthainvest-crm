# Mutual Fund Research

A source list, not a single tool - which site to check depends on what's actually being asked. Use WebFetch/WebSearch against these rather than relying on memory for anything fund-performance or price related, since that data changes daily and any cached knowledge is stale.

## Fund-specific research
- **Value Research** - fund analysis & star ratings, the standard first stop for a specific fund.
- **Morningstar** - deeper fund research, useful for a more technical breakdown.
- **Advisorkhoj** - MF data & advisor-facing tools, good for SIP/comparison illustrations.
- **RupeeVest** - MF analytics (regular plan).
- **AMFI India** - the official industry body; authoritative NAV/AUM data, not opinion.
- **MF Central** - official portfolio & transaction data (CAMS/KFintech-backed) - useful when a client's actual holdings/transactions need verifying, not just fund research.
- **Cafemutual** - industry news/regulatory updates, not fund analysis - check here for "did anything change" questions (new NFOs, SEBI rule changes, AMC news).

## Equity / stock-level research (for direct-equity clients or fund underlying-holdings checks)
- **Stock Screener**, **Trendlyne**, **Equitymaster**, **StockEdge** - fundamentals and screening.
- **TradingView** - charts and technicals.
- **UnlistedZone** - unlisted/pre-IPO shares, only relevant for that specific niche.

## Portfolio Management / Alternative Investments
- **PMS Bazaar**, **PMS AIF World** - for clients above the ticket size where PMS/AIF becomes relevant.

## Macro / valuation context
- **World PE Ratio**, **Novel Investor** - valuation benchmarking.
- **Macrotrends** - long-run historical data (100 years) for context on a fund/sector's cycle.
- **IBEF** - India industry data.
- **IMF**, **Trading Economics** - global macro.
- **Economic Outlook**, **India Macro Indicators** - India-specific macro research.
- **Lazy Portfolio ETF** - global ETF benchmarking, useful for comparing an India fund against a global passive alternative.

## Official / regulatory
- **MoSPI**, **PIB** - official government statistics and announcements.
- **Fortune India 500**, **ClearTax** - company rankings and tax-and-finance context.
- **Zerodha Pulse** - market news aggregator, good for a fast daily-news check.

## Workflow

1. Identify what's actually being asked - a specific fund's rating (Value Research/Morningstar), a stock's fundamentals (Stock Screener/Trendlyne), a macro/sector question (Trading Economics/IBEF), or "what happened in the industry" (Cafemutual).
2. Fetch the relevant source(s) live rather than answering from memory - fund ratings and NAVs are time-sensitive.
3. When giving a client-facing rationale, cite where a number came from (e.g. "Value Research 5-star, expense ratio per AMFI as of [date]") rather than presenting it as generally known.
4. This is research support, not investment advice generation - present findings, let the human (a licensed distributor) make the actual recommendation to the client.
