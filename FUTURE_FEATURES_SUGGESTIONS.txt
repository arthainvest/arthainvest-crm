🎯 RECOMMENDED NEXT FEATURE (Start This Week)

LEAD SCORING (🔴 Critical Priority)

Why:
  • 2-3 hours to implement
  • Immediate productivity boost (+30 min/day saved)
  • Differentiator vs HubSpot
  • Core to mutual fund distribution (which leads to prioritize)

Implementation:
  1. Add score field to leads
  2. Calculate score based on:
     - Budget: score += budget/10000 (capped at 30)
     - Status: new=0, contacted=10, interested=20, meeting=30, closed=40
     - Days since contact: subtract 1 per week
     - Total: 0-100
  3. Display score in leads list
  4. Sort by score option
  5. Color code: Green (80+), Yellow (50-79), Red (<50)

Benefits:
  • Know which leads to call first
  • Motivation (see progress)
  • Better time management
  • More conversions
