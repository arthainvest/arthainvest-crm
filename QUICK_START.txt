❓ QUICK ANSWERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Can employees see client money/PAN?
A: No! Automatically hidden. They see names, phone, products only.

Q: Where's the data stored?
A: Your laptop (AppData\Roaming) + browser LocalStorage

Q: Is it synced to cloud?
A: No. You export CSV and share with team weekly.

Q: Can I import from Google Sheets?
A: Yes! Export as CSV, click "Import CSV" in Clients tab.

Q: What if someone leaves the team?
A: Their data stays on their laptop. You import their CSV to master.

Q: Is it DPDP compliant?
A: Yes! Audit logs built-in, role-based access, local data.

Q: Can I add more employees later?
A: Yes! Add in "Team Members" tab, build new installer.
