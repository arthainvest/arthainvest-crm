💰 Commission Dashboard

Admin-only: View and process employee commissions

ℹ️ Admin-Only Access: You can ONLY see commission reports. No access to other modules. All commissions auto-calculated from closed deals.
💡 Commission Formula Breakdown:
Commission = (Deals Closed × Avg Deal Value) × Commission Rate

Example for Rajesh (8 deals, ₹2.8Cr total):
₹2.8Cr ÷ 8 deals = ₹35L per deal (Avg)
8 × ₹35L = ₹2.8Cr (Total Value)
₹2.8Cr × 2% = ₹5.6L Commission ✓
