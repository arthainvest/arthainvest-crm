# Weekly Cadence — a system that survives being part-time

You are solo and part-time. That is the binding constraint, and it's why this plan is small.
The failure mode isn't doing too little each week — it's doing a heroic week, then nothing for
a month. **Consistency beats intensity in a referral business.**

**The week: 5 referral asks · 15 warm touches · 1 content or session touchpoint · 1 pipeline review.**

That's roughly 3–4 hours. If a week goes badly, protect the referral asks and drop the rest —
they have the highest conversion of anything you do.

## The weekly rhythm

### Monday — 30 min · Plan
- Open `03_Pipeline_CRM.xlsx`, filter `NextActionDate` ≤ today. Those are today's calls.
- Any live row with a blank `NextAction` gets one now. No exceptions — this is the discipline that
  turns a pipeline of 12 into a pipeline of 0 when it slips.
- Pick this week's 5 referral targets from the `Referral Asks` sheet.

### Tuesday — 45 min · Existing clients (highest ROI)
- 5 clients from `01_Client_Gap_Matrix.xlsx`, highest `PriorityScore` first.
- Use template §1.1 (insurance gap). All 27 clients show no health or life cover on file.
- Log the outcome in the `Approached` / `Outcome` columns.

### Wednesday — 45 min · Referral asks
- 5 asks. Template §1.2 for clients, §1.3 for the SCB/DBS bankers.
- **Ask for one named person, not "anyone".** Specific requests get specific answers.
- Only ask after you've delivered something — a review, a statement, a settled claim.

### Thursday — 45 min · Cold list, email only
- 15 emails from the `START HERE` sheet. Template §2.1.
- Send touch 2 to anyone from ~5 days ago who didn't reply.
- No reply after touch 2 → `suppression_list.csv`. **No third touch.**

### Friday — 30 min · Content + review
- One touchpoint: a LinkedIn/WhatsApp-status post, or a step toward booking a session.
- Update the pipeline. Move anyone who replied into `03_Pipeline_CRM.xlsx` with a stage and a date.
- Check: is every live row carrying a next action and a date?

## Making referral asks land

The ask is the highest-yield activity in this business and the one most people skip.

**The structure:**
1. **Deliver something first.** A review, a statement, a settled claim, a completed transaction.
2. **Ask permission to ask.** "Can I ask you a favour?" — a small yes precedes a bigger one.
3. **Name the category, not the world.** Not "anyone who needs investments" — instead "someone at
   your company who's just been promoted", or "a family member who's just started earning".
4. **Ask for one.** One name is easy. A list is homework, and homework doesn't get done.
5. **Give them an easy out.** "No problem at all if nobody comes to mind." Raises the yes rate.
6. **Close the loop.** Tell them what happened. That's what earns the second referral.

**Track it** in the `Referral Asks` sheet — `AskedOn`, `ReferralsGiven`, `Converted`. If a client
has given two, ask them more often. If a client has given none after two asks, stop asking and
just service them well.

## What to measure

Track four numbers on one line in a notebook each Friday. Resist adding more.

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Referral asks made | 5/week | The only number that compounds |
| Warm touches | 15/week | Enough to matter, small enough to sustain |
| Replies (not sends) | — | Sends measure effort; replies measure whether the message works |
| Meetings booked | 2/week | The actual leading indicator of revenue |

**Baseline to beat:** your telecaller worked these lists cold and produced zero. Any reply rate
above zero from the START HERE list is a signal the new sequence works. Judge it after 20 sends,
not after 3.

- 120 emails → perhaps 8–15 replies → 3–5 meetings → 1–2 clients. That is a *normal* outcome
  for cold outreach done properly, not a disappointing one.
- The 27 existing clients will out-convert all 11,466 cold records. Most advisors discover this
  far too late.
- The SCB/DBS banker channel has produced 8 prospects with zero systematic effort. Imagine it
  worked deliberately.

**If you only do one thing:** run the Tuesday client hour and the Wednesday referral asks. That's
90 minutes a week, it has no compliance risk, and it will produce more business than the entire
cold database.
