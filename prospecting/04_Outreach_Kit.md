# Outreach Kit — ArthaInvest Capital

Every template here is pre-checked against `06_Compliance_Guardrails.md`: no return promises,
ARN disclosed, insurer named where relevant, opt-out present, permission-first on cold records.

**Channel order — this is the part that matters.**

```
Email (value-first, has unsubscribe)  →  WhatsApp (only to people who replied)  →  Call (only to people who engaged)
```

Your telecaller ran this in reverse — cold calls to strangers, opening with a pitch — and got
nothing. That order is the single biggest change in this kit. Warm the contact first, then call.

**Footer:** append the block from §6 of the compliance doc to every email. Not repeated in full
below; it's referenced as `[FOOTER]`.

# PART 1 — Warm outreach (start here)

These have no consent problem and the highest conversion per hour. Work these before any cold list.

## 1.1 Existing client — insurance gap

*Use with `01_Client_Gap_Matrix.xlsx`. All 27 clients show no health or life policy on file.*

> **Subject:** One gap I noticed in your file
>
> Hi {First name},
>
> I was reviewing your portfolio ahead of this quarter and realised that while your mutual fund
> investments are with us, I don't have any health or life cover recorded against your name.
>
> That may simply mean you hold it elsewhere — in which case, could you send me the policy
> details so your file is complete? It matters because if something happens, whoever handles your
> affairs will look at one folder, and I'd like that folder to be complete.
>
> If you *don't* have cover in place, it's worth 20 minutes. Medical costs are the most common
> reason people are forced to redeem investments early — which undoes the compounding you've been
> patient about.
>
> I'm licensed for both TATA and Niva Bupa health products, so I can show you options side by side
> without you filling in a dozen forms.
>
> Free Tuesday or Thursday evening?
>
> {Your name}
> [FOOTER]

**Why it works:** it opens with *their* file, not your product; gives them a legitimate non-buying
response ("I have it elsewhere") which keeps the door open; and links insurance to protecting the
investment they already made with you.

# PART 2 — Cold outreach (the `data dump` lists)

**Permission-first. The first email does not pitch.** See `06_Compliance_Guardrails.md` §2 for why.

## 2.1 Touch 1 — the permission email (HNI property buyers)

*Use with the `START HERE` sheet in `02_Master_Prospect_DB.xlsx` — 120 people, Tier A, all with email.*

> **Subject:** Was your {project} enquiry — quick question
>
> Hi {First name},
>
> I'll be upfront about how I got your details: they came to me through a real-estate contact,
> from an enquiry you made about {project name}. If that's unwelcome, reply "STOP" and I'll delete
> your details permanently — no follow-up.
>
> Still reading? Here's why I wrote.
>
> People buying at that level usually have the property decision handled, but the *financing side*
> half-done — an EMI that could be restructured, a term cover that hasn't been revisited since the
> loan, or money sitting idle between the booking and possession.
>
> I've put together a one-page Financial Organiser that lays out where everything sits on a single
> sheet. Happy to send it across — no cost, and no call unless you ask for one.
>
> Want me to send it?
>
> {Your name}
> ArthaInvest Capital
> [FOOTER]

**Why it works:** it names the data source honestly (DPDP), leads with the opt-out (which
paradoxically increases replies), pitches nothing, and asks for a tiny yes. The lead magnet is
`My Work- PDFs\Financial Organiser-ArthaInvest.xlsx`, which already exists.

## 2.2 Touch 2 — the only follow-up (send ~5 days later)

> Hi {First name},
>
> Just closing the loop on this — I'll assume it's not relevant and won't write again.
>
> If you'd like the Financial Organiser, reply with a "yes" and I'll send it over.
>
> Either way, good luck with the property.
>
> {Your name}
> [FOOTER]

**If there's no reply to touch 2 → `suppression_list.csv`. No touch 3.** Two touches is the
compliant position and the commercially correct one.

## 2.3 WhatsApp — only after they've replied to email

> Hi {First name}, {Your name} from ArthaInvest here — following up on your email about the
> Financial Organiser. Sending it across now. If anything in it raises a question, happy to talk
> it through, otherwise no obligation at all.

Keep it one-to-one and conversational. Bulk broadcast needs DLT-registered templates — see
compliance §3.

## 2.4 Call opener — only for people who engaged

> "Hi {name}, this is {your name} from ArthaInvest — we exchanged a couple of emails about the
> Financial Organiser. Is now an OK time for two minutes, or should I call back this evening?"
>
> *(if OK)* "The reason I follow up is that the sheet usually throws up one of two things — either
> a loan that's costing more than it should, or no health cover behind a big EMI. Which of those is
> closer to your situation?"

**Ask, then stop talking.** The question does the work.

# PART 4 — Objections

| They say | Say back |
|---|---|
| "I already have an advisor." | "Good — you should keep them. I'm not asking you to move anything. If a second opinion on the health cover is ever useful, I'm here." |
| "Not interested." | "Understood, I'll take you off the list. Before I go — is that not interested in investing, or not interested in being sold to? Because I'd have the same reaction to the second." |
| "What returns will I get?" | "I can't promise a number, and anyone who does is telling you something they can't know. What I *can* do is show you the range of outcomes and what the risk actually looks like." |
| "Send me details on WhatsApp." | "Will do. One question so I send the right thing — is this for growing money, protecting it, or a specific goal like a house or education?" |
| "How much do you charge?" | "For mutual funds I'm paid a commission by the AMC, disclosed in the scheme document — no separate fee from you. Insurance is the same." |
| "I'll think about it." | "Of course. Can I call you on {specific date} — or would you rather I leave it with you?" |
| "How did you get my number?" | *Answer honestly.* "Through a real-estate contact, from your {project} enquiry. If you'd rather I delete it, I will right now." |

# PART 5 — Disposition codes

Use these in the `Response` column so the data stays workable. **Give these to your telecaller** —
point them at appointment-setting on warm and referral lists, which a junior can do well, rather
than cold pitching, which they can't.

| Code | Meaning | Next |
|---|---|---|
| `INT` | Interested | Move to `03_Pipeline_CRM.xlsx`, set a NextActionDate |
| `CB` | Call back | Diarise the exact date they gave |
| `NA` | No answer | Retry once, different time of day |
| `WN` | Wrong number | Suppression list |
| `NI` | Not interested | Suppression list. Re-approach only after 6 months |
| `DNC` | Asked not to be contacted | Suppression list — **permanent, all channels** |
| `LM` | Lead magnet sent | Follow up in 5 days, once |
| `MTG` | Meeting booked | Pipeline, stage 3 |

**Golden rule:** a record with no disposition code is a record you will call twice and annoy.
