# Loan seekers — start here

Work in this order. Warmest first. **Do not open the 511-row list until the top two sections
are done** — they are worth more than the rest combined.

---

## Before you dial — three rules

1. **DND-scrub the numbers.** These are calls, not emails. Calls to DND-registered numbers
   from an unregistered sender attract penalties.
2. **Never promise approval, a rate, or a saving.** You source as a DSA; the lender decides.
   Say that out loud on the call — it builds more credibility than a promise would.
3. **The numbers are 5–8 years old.** Expect heavy disconnection. That is normal and not a
   signal the list is bad.
