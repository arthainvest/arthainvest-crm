# ICP formats + LinkedIn playbook

Four ideal customer profiles, each with copy-paste LinkedIn search strings, qualifying signals,
and the exact words to send. Built for **free LinkedIn** — no Sales Navigator needed.

---

## Read this first — the boundary that matters

**Manual search and personalised connection requests: completely fine.** That is what LinkedIn is for.

**Bulk scraping, auto-connect bots, or exporting profiles into a database: not fine.** It breaches
LinkedIn's User Agreement, and it recreates the exact DPDP consent problem that made your 11,466
old records nearly unusable. You would be rebuilding the failure, just with fresher names.

So: search manually, connect deliberately, and let people opt in. Slower, and the only version
that produces clients rather than a suppression list.

### Free LinkedIn's real limits

- **Commercial Use Limit** — free accounts get capped on people-searches per month (resets on the
  1st). You'll see a warning as you approach it. This is precisely why the search strings below
  are narrow: you cannot afford broad browsing.
- **Personalised invites are capped** on free accounts. LinkedIn changes the number periodically —
  check what yours allows before planning a week around it.
- **Connection note limit: 300 characters.** Every note below fits.

**Realistic part-time volume: 10 connection requests a week.** Not 50. Ten thoughtful ones from
someone with an ARN and a real profile beat fifty templated ones.

# ICP 2 · NRI

**Why they fit you:** your best-converting segment and least competitive. 5 of your 12 live
prospects are NRI, sourced through DBS and SCB. You already hold NRI KYC and FATCA declarations
for every major AMC — that is the actual product.

**Products:** MF via NRE/NRO, portfolio clean-up of dormant Indian folios, term cover, and
repatriation/property-sale TDS guidance.

### Qualifying signals
- Indian name, based Dubai / Singapore / London / USA / Australia / Qatar / Oman
- Studied in India (school filter is the strongest NRI proxy LinkedIn offers)
- 5+ years abroad — long enough to have accumulated, and for Indian paperwork to have gone stale
- Senior title: AVP and above, or founder

### Disqualifiers
- Recently moved abroad (nothing accumulated yet)
- OCI/foreign national with no Indian assets — different rules entirely

### LinkedIn searches
**LinkedIn has no "NRI" filter.** The reliable proxy is *location abroad + education in India*.

Set **Location = Dubai / Singapore / London**, then add **School**:
Mumbai University · IIT Bombay · NMIMS · VJTI · Sydenham College · Jai Hind College ·
St. Xavier's Mumbai · Symbiosis · SP Jain

```
("Vice President" OR "AVP" OR "Director" OR "Senior Manager" OR "Founder") AND ("Mumbai" OR "India")
```
```
("NRI" OR "Non Resident Indian" OR "Indian expat" OR "Indian diaspora")
```
Use your existing corridors — **Dubai, Singapore, UK** — because that's where your referrals
already come from and you understand those clients.

### Connection note (289 chars)
> Hello {First name} — I work with NRIs on the India side of their finances: NRE/NRO, FATCA, KYC
> that went stale after moving, and folios sitting untouched for years. Mumbai-based, used to
> working across time zones. Connecting in case it's ever useful.

### First message after they accept
> Thanks for connecting. Most NRIs I speak with aren't stuck on *what* to invest in — they're
> stuck on the admin. NRE vs NRO, whether a KYC done years ago still stands, and who handles it
> when you're in a different time zone.
>
> That's the part I take on. If you have investments in India sitting untouched since you moved,
> happy to look at what's there and tell you what needs cleaning up. What time zone are you in?

**Never** advise on their country-of-residence tax position. Say so plainly — it builds trust and
keeps you inside your licence.

# Weekly rhythm

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | 10 connection requests — one ICP per week, rotating |
| **Tue–Thu** | Reply to anyone who accepted. One message, then stop. |
| **Fri** | Log everything in `outbox\H_linkedin_tracker.csv` |
| **Ongoing** | 3 posts a week from `10_Content_Calendar.md` — this is what makes the requests get accepted |

**Content and connections compound together.** A connection request from someone with three
months of useful posts gets accepted far more often than one from an empty profile. Post first,
connect second.

# Compliance on LinkedIn

- **ARN-267891 in your profile headline or About.** Then individual posts don't each need it.
- **Never state or imply returns** — not in posts, not in DMs, not in comments.
- **Never name a scheme as a recommendation** to someone who isn't a client.
- **Don't post client details or portfolios**, even anonymised, without written permission.
- **One follow-up maximum.** No reply means no.
- Anyone who says stop goes into `suppression_list.csv` like any other channel.
