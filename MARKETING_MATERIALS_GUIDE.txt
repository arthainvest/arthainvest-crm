🎯 WHAT IS THE MARKETING MATERIALS FEATURE?

The Marketing Materials feature allows you to:
  ✅ Create and store marketing materials (brochures, guides, offers, etc.)
  ✅ Share materials with clients via WhatsApp
  ✅ Share materials with clients via Email
  ✅ Track all marketing materials in one place
  ✅ Include links to digital assets
  ✅ Select multiple clients for bulk sharing

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 HOW TO USE - STEP BY STEP

STEP 1: ACCESS MARKETING MATERIALS
  1. Login to ArthaInvest CRM
  2. Click: 📢 Marketing (in left sidebar)
  3. You'll see the Marketing Materials section

STEP 2: CREATE A NEW MARKETING MATERIAL
  1. Click: "+ Add Marketing Material" button
  2. Fill in the form:
     • Material Name: e.g., "Investment Guide 2026"
     • Material Type: Choose from dropdown
       - Brochure
       - Investment Guide
       - White Paper
       - Product Info
       - Special Offer
       - Newsletter
       - Other
     • Description: Your marketing message
     • Link (optional): URL to the material (Google Drive, website, etc.)
  3. Click: "Save Material"
  4. Success! ✅ Material saved

STEP 3: SHARE VIA WHATSAPP
  1. Find your material in the list
  2. Click: 📱 WhatsApp button
  3. A modal appears with all your clients
  4. Select clients you want to share with:
     ☑ Client 1
     ☑ Client 2
     ☑ Client 3
  5. Click: "Share via WhatsApp"
  6. WhatsApp links open for each selected client
  7. Send the message on WhatsApp

STEP 4: SHARE VIA EMAIL
  1. Find your material in the list
  2. Click: ✉️ Email button
  3. A modal appears with all your clients
  4. Select clients you want to email:
     ☑ Client 1
     ☑ Client 2
     ☑ Client 3
  5. Click: "Share via Email"
  6. Email clients open for each selected client
  7. Send the email (you can edit before sending)

💬 WHATSAPP SHARING - HOW IT WORKS

When you share via WhatsApp:

1. A WhatsApp link opens for each selected client
2. The message includes:
   • Material name
   • Your description/message
   • The link (if provided)
   • Professional signature

Example message:

  ✓ Client must have phone number saved

🔗 USING LINKS IN MATERIALS

You can include a link to your marketing material:

Recommended link sources:
  • Google Drive (shareable link)
  • OneDrive / Dropbox
  • Your company website
  • PDF hosting service
  • Marketing platform

How to get a link:
  1. Upload file to Google Drive / cloud storage
  2. Right-click → Share
  3. Change to "Anyone with link"
  4. Copy link
  5. Paste in Material Link field

Example links:
  https://drive.google.com/file/d/1abc123/view
  https://example.com/investment-guide.pdf
  https://www.brochure-link.com/2026-guide

📊 BEST PRACTICES

✅ DO:
  • Keep descriptions short and compelling
  • Include a link to the full material
  • Use appropriate material types
  • Segment clients by interest before sharing
  • Follow up with phone call after email
  • Track which clients received what

❌ DON'T:
  • Send too many materials at once
  • Use generic descriptions
  • Share without client email/phone
  • Forget to include call-to-action link
  • Share irrelevant materials
  • Spam with daily materials

📲 CLIENT INFORMATION REQUIREMENTS

For successful sharing:

WhatsApp Sharing:

Email Sharing:
  Required: Valid email address
  Example: client@company.com

❓ FAQ

Q: Can I share to multiple clients at once?
A: Yes! Select multiple clients in the share modal.

Q: Can I edit a material after saving?
A: Currently, you'll need to delete and recreate. We're adding edit feature soon.

Q: What happens if a client doesn't have phone/email?
A: The system warns you. You can edit the client's info first.

Q: Can I schedule materials for later?
A: Currently, sharing is immediate. Scheduled sending coming soon.

Q: Is there a way to see who received what?
A: Materials are tracked by creation date and creator. We're adding delivery tracking.

Q: Can I delete a material?
A: Yes! Click the 🗑️ delete button on any material. Confirm deletion.

Q: What's the character limit for descriptions?
A: No hard limit, but keep under 500 chars for best results on WhatsApp.

🚀 PRO TIPS

Tip 1: Personalization
  Address clients by name in WhatsApp
  Subject lines with client names in email

Tip 2: Timing
  Send WhatsApp during business hours
  Send emails on Tuesday-Thursday mornings

Tip 3: Segmentation
  Group clients by interest
  Share relevant materials only

Tip 4: Call-to-Action
  Include clear next step in description
  "Call me to discuss" or "Reply with questions"

Tip 5: Follow-up
  Send via WhatsApp first (faster delivery)
  Follow up with email (official record)
