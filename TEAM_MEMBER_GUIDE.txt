================================================================================
  HOW TO ADD A LEAD (Most Important!)
================================================================================

Step-by-step to add a new prospect:

1. Click "Leads" in the left sidebar
2. You see a form at the top:

   Fill in these fields:
   ├─ Name: [Prospect's full name]
   ├─ Phone: [Their phone number]
   ├─ Email: [Their email address]
   ├─ Company: [Their company name]
   ├─ Source: [How you found them]
   │  Options: Direct, Referral, LinkedIn, Website, Other
   ├─ Product: [What product they need]
   │  Options: Term Insurance, Health Insurance, POSP, DSA
   └─ Budget: [₹ Amount they're willing to spend]

3. Click: "Add Lead"
4. Success message will appear
5. Scroll down to see your new lead in the table

IMPORTANT: The system auto-calculates a SCORE (0-100) and TIER:
   ├─ HOT (80+): Call today!
   ├─ WARM (60-79): Schedule meeting
   ├─ COOL (40-59): Follow up next week
   └─ COLD (<40): Long-term prospect

The score is based on:
   - Budget amount
   - Company information
   - How they found you
   - Engagement level

================================================================================
  HOW TO LOG A COMMUNICATION
================================================================================

After every call, email, or meeting, log it:

1. Click "Communications" in sidebar
2. Fill in:
   ├─ Contact Name: [Person you talked to]
   ├─ Channel: [How you communicated]
   │  Options: Phone, Email, WhatsApp, In-person
   ├─ Type: [What kind of communication]
   │  Options: Outbound Call, Inbound Call, Email, Meeting
   ├─ Subject: [Brief topic]
   ├─ Outcome: [How it went]
   │  Options: Positive, Neutral, Negative
   └─ Notes: [Any important details]

3. Click: "Log Communication"

WHY LOG COMMUNICATIONS?
   - Never forget what you discussed
   - Track next follow-up dates
   - See communication history
   - Team can see your progress
   - Helps with deal status updates

================================================================================
  COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: I can't find a lead I added yesterday
A: Use Ctrl+F to search. Or scroll in the leads table.

Q: The score didn't calculate
A: Refresh the page (F5). It takes ~10 seconds.

Q: What does the score mean?
A: 0-100 scale. Higher = more qualified. 80+ = HOT (call today!)

Q: Can I edit a lead after adding it?
A: Currently view-only. Contact your manager for edits.

Q: What if I make a mistake entering a lead?
A: Contact your manager - they can delete/edit in admin.

Q: Why do I see other people's data?
A: Depending on your role. Employee sees all data. This is normal.

Q: Can I export the data?
A: Ask your manager - admin can export to Excel.

Q: The app crashed
A: Restart it. If it keeps crashing, contact your manager.

Q: How often does data backup?
A: Automatically on your computer. Never lost.
